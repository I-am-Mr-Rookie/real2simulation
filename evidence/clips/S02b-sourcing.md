Each page identifies an independent stock clip and creator; [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
allows use and modification and does not require attribution (credits retained
anyway). Restrictions include no implied endorsement and no unaltered stock
redistribution. Check depicted rights, third-party content, blur, camera translation,
parallax, stable floor/planes and independent scale; swift spin is a reconstruction
risk. Room source dimensions/FPS are page claims, not measured metadata. No
guarantee that any one scene meets Gate R or has a known-size object.
