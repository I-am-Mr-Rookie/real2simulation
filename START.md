# Start here (Koushik)

1. Open **RUNS.md** and look at "Ready now".
2. Open the tool it names (Codex or Claude Code) and set the model and effort shown.
3. Say: `Complete run S05a`, or just `Complete the next run`.
4. The agent does the rest: the work, the checks, the commits, and ticking RUNS.md.
5. Run at most 2 at once, never two from the same lane.

Runs ask you questions in the run itself (pick an option or type your own) and keep working while you decide.
Unanswered ones, and questions from orchestrator mode, collect under "Questions for Koushik" in RUNS.md.
Your own items are under "Waiting for Koushik" in RUNS.md.
To mark one of yours done, tell any agent, for example "S02e is done: clip list approved".

Busy? Open the agent you trust most and say: "Read ORCHESTRATOR.md and be the orchestrator."
