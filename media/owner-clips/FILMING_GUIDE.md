# N06: filming guide for Koushik (tonight, about 30 minutes)

Put the files in "Videos for Meta AI Hackathon/" with these names. Phone landscape, 1080p, 60 fps if your phone
offers it (30 fps is fine). Good light, no faces, no screens with personal info in view.

| File | What to film | How |
|---|---|---|
| N06_room.mp4 | Your room for the virtual lab | 30 to 40 s. Lay an A4 sheet flat on the floor in view. Walk slowly along one side, phone at chest height, pan gently; overlap what you already filmed; finish where you started. No people. |
| N06_pendulum.mp4 | Pendulum | Phone on a stand (books work), not moving. A small heavy object on a string, 40 to 80 cm long. Measure the string length if you can and say it in the file name, e.g. N06_pendulum_62cm.mp4. Pull it to the side, release, film 10 to 15 s. Plain background, A4 sheet or ruler in the same plane. |
| N06_ramp.mp4 | Ball rolling down a ramp | Phone on a stand, side view. A board or book propped up as a ramp, ball released from rest, roll to the floor. 5 to 10 s. Ruler or A4 sheet in the same plane. |
| N06_spin.mp4 (optional) | Something spinning | A wheel, fidget spinner or plate with a clear mark on it, spun by hand and left to slow down. Top or side view, phone fixed, 10 s. |

Tips: hold the phone still for experiments (stand, not hand); keep the whole motion in frame; contrast helps
(dark object on a light background). One good take of each is enough. Reply "N06 done" in RUNS.md or chat.
These clips are for the product demo and the video; tell me which ones may appear publicly.
