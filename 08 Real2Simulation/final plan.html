Target: Real2Simulation turns a video (a licensed public clip in the demo library, or the user's own upload) into a measurable 3D physics world. Real motion in the clip is measured (g, launch speed, friction, restitution); those values calibrate the simulation; the agent predicts the part of the clip it has not seen, and the app overlays prediction against reality. A virtual lab with realistic instrument error and a Mystery-g mode covers rooms without motion. It also has a demo library, a sidebar agent and lab reports.
