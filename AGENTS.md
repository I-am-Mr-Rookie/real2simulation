## Product direction (Nov 1 pivot; plan: [Run-System/PLAN-NOV1.md](Run-System/PLAN-NOV1.md))
Signed-in users upload their own videos and the system handles them live. Two modes chosen by the agent's first
question: (A) room video -> live reconstruction -> editable virtual lab with physics and agent-run experiments;
(B) own phone experiment -> live tracking -> fit with uncertainty -> 3D replay and "redo with changes".
Hosted Meta SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API) is the default for segmentation/tracking; Modal GPU is the backup and runs
MapAnything / 3D lifting on demand. Recorded replays are only sample projects and an outage fallback.

## Honesty (unchanged)
Numbers come only from code, the fitter or model/API output, never model-authored. Keep labels: RECORDED,
SIMULATED, MEASURED, MODEL-ESTIMATED, what-if, scale unverified. Always show uncertainty. Imperfect inputs are
used with explicit caveats instead of abstaining. No em dashes in visible text.
