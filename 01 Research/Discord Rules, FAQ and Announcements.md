## #rules

**Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon — Rules**

Read these before you build. Breaking them can disqualify your project.

**New work only.** Everything you present has to be built during the hackathon, Oct 26 to Nov 4.

**Identify your contributions.** Your demo has to show only the features, code and functionality your team built during the event. Judges need to be able to tell what's new. If they can't, the project is disqualified.

**Public repo.** Your submitted repository must be public.

**Teams.** Max 4 people, solo is allowed. Pick one track, Indie Developer or Startup, and enter the project in that track only. One person submits for the team.

**Demo video.** 3 minutes, required on the submission. It's one of the main things judges score.

**Banned projects.** Disqualified if they break legal, ethical or platform policies, or use code, data or assets you don't have the rights to. Also avoid the generic stuff such as AI medical or health advice, Basic RAG apps, Simple image analyzers, "AI for education" chatbots, Job application screeners, Nutrition coaches, Personality analyzers, Sports analyzers or coaches

**Theme.** Your project has to fit one of the two problem statements:
- **Build for Your World** — bring Meta's models into a domain you know and solve a real problem in it.
- **Push the Frontier** — use Muse Spark, Glimmer, SAM 3 or other Meta models on hard problems in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy or science.

**Submit** by Wed Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET: https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit
Public repo, working demo video link, every team member added.

**Also in the waiver you agreed to:** you must be 18+ and of legal age where you live, and Meta or CV staff, contractors, government-affiliated people and their households can't participate.

## #faqs

### FAQ — Getting started

**What are we building?** Your project must use Meta models and address one of these challenges:
- Build for Your World: Solve a problem you know firsthand.
- Push the Frontier: Bring new capabilities to software, robotics, science, manufacturing, energy or other real-world systems.

**Which models can I use?** The guide lists Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1 through Meta Model API, plus open-weight Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open. Muse Code is also available to help you code.

**Can I enter solo?** Yes! Teams can have up to 4 people.

**What are the key dates?** All times are Eastern Time (ET).
- Oct 26, noon: Kickoff.
- Oct 27–Nov 4: Building period.
- Nov 4, 9 PM: Submission deadline.
- Nov 5–8: Judging.
- Nov 9, noon: Closing ceremony, live finals and main winners.
- Nov 16: Community vote winner announced.

### FAQ — Project rules

**Can I build on an existing project or startup?** Yes, but your submission and demo must clearly identify—and only showcase—the features, code and functionality built during the hackathon. Unclear original contributions can lead to disqualification.

**Does my code need to be public?** Yes. Your repository must be publicly accessible.

**What projects should I avoid?** The guide specifically lists:
- AI medical advice, mental-health advisors and skin-condition detectors.
- Basic RAG apps, simple image analyzers and generic education chatbots.
- Job-application screeners, nutrition coaches and personality analyzers.
- Sports analyzers or coaches.
- Anything political or involving war.

Projects that violate legal, ethical or platform policies, or use code, data or assets without permission, will be disqualified. Check with @CV if you're unsure about your idea.

**How will projects be judged?** First, projects must fit the theme and meaningfully use Meta Model API and/or Meta open-weight models. Eligible entries are then scored equally on:
- Impact
- Use of Meta AI tooling
- Technical depth and execution
- Demo quality

The top 5 projects per competition track advance to the finals.

### FAQ — Credits and support

**Do participants get API credits?** Accepted participants receive $150 in Meta Model API credits, claimed individually—not on behalf of teammates. Claim by Nov 4, 9 PM ET.

**When will credits arrive?** Credits are distributed in batches. Check your account's Billing page before using them.

**Do I need a credit card?** The guide says a card is required to use Meta API credits and make API calls. Monitor your usage: spending beyond the credits may incur charges. Don't assume a "Spend Limit" notification automatically stops billing.

**Are partner credits available?**
- Baseten: Apply for $100 and a 2M-token/minute rate limit. Up to 2,000 participants; approval depends on use case.
- Modal: $100 for the first 1,000 participants.
- Fireworks: Details TBC.

**Which Baseten model ID should I use?** Use `meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026` for this hackathon.

**Where can I get help?** General questions (tag @CV); #office-hours — Meta team support, weekdays 10–11 AM ET during the building period; Baseten channel; #announcements — official updates.

### FAQ — Submissions, prizes and resources

**How do I submit?** Choose one team representative to submit one project per team/startup through the submission platform. Include a 3-minute demo video, a public repository and every contributor's name. Check that your video and repository links work. Judges assess your video, code and live product. Deadline: Nov 4 at 9 PM ET. Late submissions won't be considered.

**What are the prizes?** Indie Developer: $150k / $100k / $75k / $50k / $25k. Startups: $250k / $150k / $75k / $50k / $25k. Bonus: Most Viral Project $20k; Best Build on Meta Open Weight Models $10k; Community Vote $10k; Best Agentic Use-Case $5k; Best Creative Build $5k.

**How do the public awards work?** For Most Viral, post your demo on X and submit the post link through the platform; engagement counts through Nov 8 EOD. Community voting opens Nov 9, with one vote per verified account.

**Where should I start learning?** Quickstart (https://dev.meta.ai/docs/quickstart), model list (https://dev.meta.ai/docs/models), API cookbook (https://github.com/meta-models/meta-model-cookbook), open-weight cookbook (https://github.com/meta-models/meta-oss-cookbook). Live sessions: Oct 27 Agents 101; Oct 28 Agents 102; Oct 30 Fireworks inference and training. All noon–1 PM ET.

### Organiser answers (Kai | CV, Oct 26)

**Project collaborators.** Everyone involved in the creation process of the project must be an approved participant. Teams are formed only from approved participants; no more approvals are possible.

**Using a prior codebase.** The submitted repo must be a standalone extract: a fresh repo containing only work done during the event, keeping the existing codebase out of it. Open the 3-minute video with one line stating what was pre-existing and what was built this week.

**Billing.** A valid payment method triggers a temporary pre-authorization charge, released automatically within a few business days. A "restricted" card error means the card failed Meta's payment bar; a new card is needed.

**Correction:** the building period officially starts after the kickoff session.
